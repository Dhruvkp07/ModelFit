# Community conduct

ModelFit welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be constructive, respectful, patient with newcomers, and specific when reporting bugs or reviewing code. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove violating content and restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to a maintainer via a GitHub contact channel where possible; do not publish sensitive personal information in public Issues.

We aim to judge contributions by clarity, correctness, maintainability, and usefulness rather than seniority.
